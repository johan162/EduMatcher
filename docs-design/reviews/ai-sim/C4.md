# WP-C4 — the tuning chapter's recipes, measured

Plan: [EduMatcher-AI-Sim-Book-Plan.md](../../EduMatcher-AI-Sim-Book-Plan.md), WP-C4.
Chapter: `docs/books/ai-traders/part-2-how-it-works/060-tuning-the-traders.md`.

## Method

The harness in [`harness/`](harness/) (as run, with the cloud container's
paths): `recipes.py NAME...` builds a fresh data directory per recipe with
its own `engine_config.yaml`, `market_sim.yaml`, `swarm.yaml` and `ai-sim`
profile (base: the shipped `ai-sim` profile — symmetric market maker,
budget 80), starts the real `entrypoint.sh`, observes with `simwatch2.py`
(one row a minute from the engine and `pm-market-sim` PUB sockets) and
stops it. `summ.py` averages the continuous-trading rows after the first
four. Real-speed runs: simulated Monday from 09:28, 16 minutes. Day runs:
`EM_SPEED=24` from 08:58, 21 minutes (a whole trading day). Same seeds
throughout. Auction volume from `stats.db` `trade_log` rows with
`aggressor_side = AUCTION`.

Columns: RMS and median of |ln(mid/value)| in %, symbols > 2 % off,
RMS one-minute mid return in %, median relative spread in %, trades a
minute, symbols traded per minute and per 5 minutes, CPU % of one core.

## What changed in the chapter

- Every recipe's "what you should see" replaced by the measured outcome.
- **Efficient market** promised "prices glued to the values"; measured: no
  change (0.32 % vs 0.29 %). Kept, re-described as the ceiling.
- **Auction lesson** had `budget: 30`, which *shrank* the opening auction
  (11 176 shares vs 15 216); dropped. `auction-player: 20` alone gives
  +58 % opening-auction volume and 298/300 symbols crossing.
- **News day** and **rumour mill** cannot be measured at real speed in a
  short run: both real-speed runs (`newsday`, `rumourmill`, with
  `rate_symbol: 60`) published no headline at all in 16 minutes (about two
  expected; the generator is seeded, so every short run from the same start
  replays the same draws). They were run over a whole day at `EM_SPEED=24`.
- Removing the market maker **narrows** the median spread (0.03 % vs
  0.10 %; the AI traders compete for the touch) while prices move three
  times further from the values; the chapter and chapter 100 said spreads
  widen — corrected.

## Acceptance

Each recipe's promised effect measured and quoted: **met**. The plan's
"script kept in `tools/` or tests" is kept as evidence under `harness/`
instead: it drives the real container entrypoint with container-specific
paths and runs for hours, so it is not a test.
