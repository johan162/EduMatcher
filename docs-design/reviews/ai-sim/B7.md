# WP-B7 — soak: a month of trading days, unattended

Plan: [EduMatcher-AI-Sim-Book-Plan.md](../../EduMatcher-AI-Sim-Book-Plan.md), WP-B7.

## Set-up

Cloud container, 2 vCPU, 8 GB. The real `deployment/docker/entrypoint.sh`
against a fresh data directory: `EM_CONFIG=s300-sim`, `EM_PROFILE=ai-sim`
(symmetric market maker, swarm budget 80), `EM_RESTART_DEAD=1`,
`TZ=Europe/Stockholm`, `EM_SPEED=600`, `EM_START=2026-10-12T08:50`. Observed
for 110 wall minutes by the same once-a-minute observer as [B1](B1.md).

## First attempt: the market maker never came back

The first soak start lost `pm-mm-bot` at once and the watchdog restarted it
every minute for good:

1. It crashed at start-up with an uncaught `zmq.Again`: its first send,
   `gateway_connect`, 50 ms after connecting a PUSH socket that never blocks
   (`SNDTIMEO 0`, `IMMEDIATE`), found no connection yet.
2. Every restart then exited 1 with `auth rejected: Gateway already
   connected: MM01`. `pm-mm-bot` never sent `system.gateway_heartbeat` nor
   `system.gateway_disconnect`, so the engine kept `MM01` connected for ever
   once a bot had died without logging off (the engine only times out
   sessions that heartbeat). The same held for a plain `SIGTERM`: stopping
   and restarting the market maker alone could never log on again until the
   engine restarted.

Fixed in `src/edumatcher/mm_bot/bot.py`: the connect is retried while the
engine is unreachable (within the existing 3 s auth timeout); after the auth
is accepted the bot heartbeats the engine at once and every
`GATEWAY_HEARTBEAT_INTERVAL_SEC` (60 s); `run()` sends
`gateway_disconnect` on every exit after a successful auth. Five tests in
`tests/test_mm_bot.py::TestEngineSession`. The soak was restarted on the
fixed code.

## Disk at real speed (from the B1 `final` run)

Per-row sizes measured in `stats.db`/`clearing.db` (indexes included):
trade_log 157 B, order_events 320 B, clearing trade_events 280 B. At the
steady 1 400 trades a minute and 6.5-hour days:

| File | Per trading day | Kept | Cap |
|---|---|---|---|
| stats trades + snapshots | ≈ 85 MB | 7 calendar days (≈ 5 trading) | ≈ 0.45 GB |
| stats order events | ≈ 250 MB/hour | 72 minutes | ≈ 0.3 GB |
| clearing trades | ≈ 155 MB | 7 calendar days, pruned at close | ≈ 0.8 GB |
| log.db | ≈ 5 MB | 30 days | ≈ 150 MB |
| audit | — | 60 MB | 60 MB |

30-day projection: about 2 GB; the book asks for 4 GB free.
