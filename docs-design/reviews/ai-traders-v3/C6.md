# C6 — 30-day soak

Container. `s150-nominal`, 20 agents, `pm-scheduler --daily --speed 1200
--start 2026-12-07T08:55`: trading days 2026-12-07 … 2027-01-20 (weekends and
holidays skipped). `pm-engine` `kill -9` on day 10, `pm-alf-gwy` `kill -9` on
day 20.

| Measure | Result |
|---|---|
| Process exits | none (other than the two kills) |
| Daily summaries | all 30 days × 20 agents |
| Worker RSS day 5 → day 30 | 36 672 KB → 36 824 KB (+0.4 %) |
| Rejects | 4 `INTERNAL_ERROR` = `ENGINE_UNAVAILABLE` during the engine restart |

Compressed days are short (about 21 s of continuous trading), so about 130
orders a day: order ages and decision rates run on the wall clock.

Bugs found by the soak and fixed:

- epoll fd reuse: a reconnect got the same fd number, the selector skipped
  re-registering it, and the session never became ready. The worker now
  compares connection objects (regression test proves the old logic fails).
- Agents stepped while their session was down and recorded phantom submits;
  `Transport.is_up` now gates them.
- The engine's start-up broadcast is often missed (slow joiner): `pm-alf-gwy`
  now probes the engine's gateway list every 5 s and drops sessions the
  engine no longer holds; the worker re-reads the market until the session
  status answers.
- `_expire_silent_gateways` ran every loop (9 % engine CPU at 500 sessions);
  now once a second.

Findings (pre-existing, documented, not fixed): a running `pm-scheduler`
does not notice an engine restart mid-day (the market stays closed until the
next day; restart the scheduler); scheduler `SIGTERM` can take up to 30 s;
`python-holidays` SE has no 24 or 31 December holidays.
