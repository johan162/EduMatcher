# E4 / E6 — news-reactive trading and the classroom scenarios

Container. `s150-nominal` (generated news switched off in the deployed
`market_sim.yaml`), `pm-engine`, `pm-alf-gwy`, `pm-market-sim`, `pm-ai-swarm`
with the example's `swarm.yaml` (20 agents, budget 40, every agent on all 150
symbols), continuous trading without a countdown (6.5-hour model day).
Headlines sent with `pm-news` (E4) and `pm-news play` of the three shipped
scenarios (E6). Mids sampled from book snapshots, values from `sim.value`.
`pm-mm-bot` was started as well but exited at start-up (the bug at the end
of this file), so the example's seeded quotes were the only market making.

## First run — criterion failed, design flaw found

Confirmed AAPL news with impact +0.0953: the value rose 10.1 %, the mid
**+0.03 %** in 60 s. Every crossing order was priced one or two ticks past the
touch, so a 10 % move needed about a thousand orders, and the passive market
maker kept refilling the old price. No amount of news-trader activity could
have met the criterion.

Fix: a strategy's intent carries a limit price. A `value` agent pays up to its
view of the value less its threshold; a `news` agent up to
`move × conviction` beyond the price it saw when it first acted on the
headline (anchored, so its own buying does not ratchet the target).
Marketable, IOC and FOK orders are priced at that limit.

## Re-run (with the fix)

| Criterion | Result |
|---|---|
| Confirmed +10 % symbol news → mid ≥ +7 % within 60 s | AAPL +7.45 % after ~15 s, **+8.80 %** at 60 s (value +10.1 %) — pass |
| Rumour (credibility 0.5, sentiment +0.7) then retraction → mid within ±2 % of pre-rumour within 10 min | MSFT peaked +3.88 % on the rumour; within 2 % 5 s after the retraction; **+1.67 %** at +10 min (+1.42 pp of it is the value's own drift) — pass |
| Sector news moves the sector more than the rest | UTILITIES (−0.05 impact) mean −4.43 % in 60 s vs rest +0.01 % — pass |

## E6 — shipped scenarios, played during continuous trading

Mean mid and value change of the scenario's symbols, from the start of the
play (the headline comes at 60 s):

| Scenario | What happened | Exercise checkpoint |
|---|---|---|
| `earnings-surprise` | value +8.1 % at the headline; AAPL mid +6.4 % within 30 s | met |
| `rumour-confirmed` | JPM mid +1.35 % on the rumour, value unchanged; at the confirmation (240 s) value +12.0 %, mid +5.0 % within 75 s | met |
| `rumour-retracted` | ENERGY mid −3.1 % on the rumour, values unchanged; after the retraction (240 s) back to ±0.3 % of the value within a few minutes (brief +1.3 % overshoot) | met |

All three `pm-news play` runs exited 0.

Found while preparing E6 and fixed: `pm-news play` started during continuous
trading never fired its continuous-phase steps (see [E3](E3.md)).

Found in this run and fixed (pre-existing): `pm-mm-bot` exited with code 1
when started while the session sat in one phase — it waited only for a
`session.state` broadcast, which the engine sends on transitions. It now also
asks for the session status. Verified: started against a `CLOSED` engine, it
stays up.

## Re-run with the `ai-swarm` profile's market maker

After the D5 findings the `ai-swarm` profile's `pm-mm-bot` quotes around the
true values (`--anchor-sim 0.3`, with mm-demo's 5 s re-issue delay and
3-tick / 20 s fade). Same scenario, that market maker running:

| Criterion | Result |
|---|---|
| Confirmed +10 % → mid ≥ +7 % within 60 s | +6.5 % after 5 s, +9.95 % after 15 s, **+10.09 %** at 60 s — pass |
| Rumour then retraction → within ±2 % in 10 min | MSFT peaked +2.82 %; **+0.12 %** at +10 min — pass |
| Sector news | UTILITIES −3.60 % vs rest +0.02 % — pass |

| Scenario | Result |
|---|---|
| `earnings-surprise` | AAPL +8.1 % within 30 s of the headline (value +8.1 %) |
| `rumour-confirmed` | JPM flat on the rumour; +12.0 % within 30 s of the confirmation |
| `rumour-retracted` | ENERGY at most −0.6 % on the rumour |

A market maker quoting around the true value sells to every rumour buyer at
the old price, so rumours leave little trace (a weaker anchor, 0.05, gave the
same picture). Training exercises 6 and 7 therefore stop the market maker
first; without it (the first re-run above) JPM rose 1.35 % on its rumour and
ENERGY fell 3.1 % on its rumour and recovered after the retraction.

Observed: an anchored `pm-mm-bot` on 150 symbols with the default 3-tick
drift re-quotes on most model steps and used ~95 % of a core; with the
profile's 15-tick drift ~70 %.
