For instructors

A class-sized market,
on demand

An exchange that is never empty

A single container runs 100 AI traders across 300 symbols, with a hidden true value, headlines and rumours. Students trade next to the bots and see prices react to news they can read.

Accelerated time

Compress a trading day into minutes so a lecture can include an opening auction, a news shock and a close.

One URL per student

API keys map to participants and roles. Give each student a trader key; put TapeDeck on the projector; keep the Operator Dashboard on your own screen.

Grade the evidence

The audit trail and clearing P&L record exactly what each participant did — perfect for post-session debriefs and assignments.
